You're about to transition this install from demo mode to real-receiver mode.

This is the right move when you have an ADS-B receiver on your network (readsb, dump1090-fa, tar1090, PiAware, or any compatible source) that you're ready to connect to. Aerodrome will stop the synthetic feeder, clear out the simulated data, and start polling your real receiver within about a minute.

⚠ This will permanently delete the demo database, watchlist entries, and stats.
The simulated aircraft history, the auto-seeded demo watchlist, and any stats records that accumulated during your demo session will be removed. This cannot be undone. After this, the dashboard will be empty until your real receiver starts reporting aircraft (typically within one poll cycle, ~60 seconds).

If you're not ready, click "Back to Configuration" in the header — your demo session keeps running until you complete this wizard.
