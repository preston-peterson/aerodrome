About Aerodrome

Self-hosted ADS-B aircraft tracker

About

Aerodrome is a self-hosted ADS-B aircraft tracker. It polls a local receiver, stores sightings in SQLite, and serves a dashboard for live traffic, watchlists, military aircraft, statistics, and historical sightings.

Source code, issues, and feature requests: github.com/preston-peterson/aerodrome.
